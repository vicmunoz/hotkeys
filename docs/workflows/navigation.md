# Navigation

| Action | Amethyst | Kitty | Neovim | Vimium |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Previous/left item | `mod1+h` or `mod2+h` depending context | ++hyper+h++ | ++ctrl+w++ then ++left++ | ++shift+h++ for browser history |
| Next/right item | `mod1+l` or `mod2+l` depending context | ++hyper+l++ | ++ctrl+w++ then ++right++ | ++shift+l++ for browser history |
| Previous/down item | ++alt+shift+j++ | ++hyper+j++ | ++ctrl+w++ then ++down++ | ++shift+j++ previous tab |
| Next/up item | ++alt+shift+k++ | ++hyper+k++ | ++ctrl+w++ then ++up++ | ++shift+k++ next tab |
